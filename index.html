Bǐlì de Jiǎkéchóng

比利的甲壳虫

Billy's Beetle

Scene 1 · Opening 开场

从cóng前qián，有yǒu一yí个ge小xiǎo男nán孩hái叫jiào比Bǐ利lì。他tā的de甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng住zhù在zài火huǒ柴chái盒hé里lǐ。
Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Billy. His beetle lived in a matchbox.
有yǒu一yì天tiān，比Bǐ利lì去qù散sàn步bù。哎āi呀yā！甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng丢diū了le！傻shǎ傻shǎ的de比Bǐ利lì！
One day, Billy went for a walk. Oh no! The beetle was lost! Silly Billy!

Scene 2 · The Search 寻找

比Bǐ利lì找zhǎo啊a找zhǎo，找zhǎo啊a找zhǎo，找zhǎo不bu到dào甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng。
Billy searched and searched and searched, but he could not find the beetle.
他tā看kàn见jiàn一yí个ge男nán人rén和hé一yì只zhī狗gǒu。狗gǒu闻wén啊a闻wén，找zhǎo不bu到dào甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng。
He met a man with a sniffy dog. The dog sniffed and sniffed, but could not find the beetle.
他tā们men看kàn见jiàn一yì只zhī刺cì猬wei，身shēn上shang有yǒu尖jiān尖jiān的de刺cì。刺cì猬wei找zhǎo啊a找zhǎo，找zhǎo不bu到dào甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng。
They met a hedgehog with sharp prickles. The hedgehog searched and searched, but could not find the beetle.

Scene 3 · Meeting Friends 遇见朋友

他tā们men看kàn见jiàn一yí位wèi老lǎo奶nǎi奶nai，她tā有yǒu软ruǎn软ruǎn的de香xiāng肠cháng。哎āi呀yā！狗gǒu狗gǒu偷tōu吃chī了le香xiāng肠cháng！
They met an old lady with some squidgy sausages. Oh no! The dog gobbled up the sausages!
他tā们men看kàn见jiàn一yì只zhī北běi极jí熊xióng、一yì支zhī乐yuè队duì和hé一yì头tóu很hěn大dà很hěn大dà的de大dà象xiàng。大dà家jiā找zhǎo啊a找zhǎo，找zhǎo不bu到dào甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng。
They met a polar bear, a band and an enormous elephant. Everyone searched and searched, but could not find the beetle.

Scene 4 · The Ending 结尾

大dà家jiā坐zuò下xià来lai，很hěn难nán过guò。聪cōng明ming的de刺cì猬wei看kàn一yi看kàn：上shàng！下xià！中zhōng间jiān！
Sadly, everyone sat down. The clever hedgehog looked — up! Down! In between!
哇wa！找zhǎo到dào了le！甲jiǎ壳ké虫chóng在zài狗gǒu狗gǒu的de头tóu上shang！万wàn岁suì！大dà家jiā都dōu很hěn开kāi心xīn。
Wow! Found it! The beetle was on the dog's head! Hooray! Everyone was happy.

Pàdīngdùn Yóulǎn Lúndūn

帕丁顿游览伦敦

Paddington Visits London

Scene 1 · Opening 开场

从cóng前qián，有yǒu一yì只zhī小xiǎo熊xióng叫jiào帕Pà丁dīng顿dùn，他tā和hé布Bù朗lǎng先xiān生sheng、布Bù朗lǎng太tài太tai住zhù在zài温Wēn莎shā花Huā园yuán。
Once upon a time, there was a little bear called Paddington who lived with Mr and Mrs Brown on Windsor Gardens.
一yì天tiān下xià午wǔ，他tā们men出chū发fā去qù伦Lún敦dūn看kàn大Dà本běn钟zhōng。
Early one afternoon, they set off across London to see Big Ben.

Scene 2 · The Journey 出发啦

首shǒu先xiān，他tā们men走zǒu过guò灰huī蒙méng蒙méng的de房fáng子zi。房fáng子zi很hěn高gāo，窗chuāng户hu很hěn长cháng。
First, they walked past the dusty houses. They were tall with long windows.
接jiē着zhe，他tā们men穿chuān过guò吵chǎo闹nào的de公gōng园yuán。孩hái子zi们men在zài秋qiū千qiān上shang大dà叫jiào。
Next, they walked through the noisy park. There were screaming children on the swings.
后hòu来lái，他tā们men沿yán着zhe浑hún浊zhuó的de河hé走zǒu。河hé水shuǐ闻wén起qǐ来lai像xiàng臭chòu鸡jī蛋dàn。
After that, they walked along the murky river. It smelled like rotten eggs.

Scene 3 · Big Ben 大本钟

最zuì后hòu，他tā们men到dào了le大Dà本běn钟zhōng。大Dà本běn钟zhōng正zhèng好hǎo敲qiāo了le三sān下xià。当dāng！当dāng！当dāng！
Finally, they arrived at Big Ben just as it chimed three times. Bong! Bong! Bong!
真zhēn幸xìng运yùn，到dào了le吃chī点diǎn心xin的de时shí间jiān，帕Pà丁dīng顿dùn大dà口kǒu吃chī了le一yí个ge橘jú子zi酱jiàng三sān明míng治zhì。
Luckily, it was time for a snack so Paddington gobbled up a marmalade sandwich.

Jiémǔ yǔ Xiāndòu

杰姆与仙豆

Jim and the Beanstalk

Scene 1 · The Problem 问题

很hěn久jiǔ很hěn久jiǔ以yǐ前qián，云yún层céng上shàng面mian住zhù着zhe一yí个ge悲bēi伤shāng的de巨jù人rén。他tā丢diū了le所suǒ有yǒu的de东dōng西xi，还hái丢diū了le假jiǎ牙yá。
A long time ago, above the clouds lived a sad giant. He lost everything, and he lost his false teeth.

Scene 2 · Jim Arrives 杰姆来了

一yì天tiān，有yǒu人rén敲qiāo门mén。门mén外wài站zhàn着zhe一yí个ge juicy 的de小xiǎo男nán孩hái。巨jù人rén说shuō：请qǐng进jìn，你nǐ很hěn安ān全quán。
One day, there was a knock. A juicy boy stood outside. The giant said: Come in, you are safe.
巨jù人rén哭kū了le：我wǒ丢diū了le假jiǎ牙yá，只zhǐ能néng喝hē汤tāng。你nǐ能néng帮bāng我wǒ吗ma？
The giant cried: I lost my false teeth, I can only drink soup. Can you help me?

Scene 3 · The Dentist 牙医

杰Jié姆mǔ量liáng了le巨jù人rén的de嘴zuǐ巴ba，拿ná着zhe金jīn币bì跑pǎo去qù找zhǎo牙yá医yī。
Jim measured the giant's mouth, took the gold coin, and ran to find the dentist.
巨jù人rén有yǒu了le新xīn牙yá齿chǐ！他tā跳tiào上shàng跳tiào下xià，开kāi心xīn地de说shuō：太tài好hǎo了le！我wǒ饿è了le！
The giant had new teeth! He jumped up and down and said happily: Wonderful! I'm hungry!
巨jù人rén看kàn着zhe杰Jié姆mǔ说shuō：炸zhà男nán孩hái！
The giant looked at Jim and said: Fried boy!

Scene 4 · Escape 逃跑

杰Jié姆mǔ赶gǎn快kuài跑pǎo！他tā滑huá下xià仙xiān豆dòu，拿ná起qǐ斧fǔ头tóu，砍kǎn倒dǎo了le仙xiān豆dòu。
Jim ran quickly! He slid down the beanstalk, grabbed an axe, and chopped it down.
天tiān上shàng掉diào下xià来lai一yì张zhāng大dà纸zhǐ，写xiě着zhe谢xiè谢xie，还hái有yǒu一yí块kuài大dà金jīn币bì。
A huge piece of paper fell from the sky saying thank you, and a giant gold coin.

Míshī yǔ Zhǎodào

迷失与找到

Lost and Found

Scene 1 · The Penguin 企鹅

从cóng前qián有yǒu一yí个ge男nán孩hái，有yǒu一yì天tiān他tā在zài门mén口kǒu发fā现xiàn了le一yì只zhī企qǐ鹅é。
Once there was a boy, and one day he found a penguin at his door.
男nán孩hái不bù知zhī道dào它tā从cóng哪nǎ里lǐ来lái。他tā决jué定dìng帮bāng企qǐ鹅é找zhǎo家jiā。
The boy didn't know where it came from. He decided to help the penguin find home.

Scene 2 · Asking for Help 寻求帮助

他tā去qù了le失shī物wù招zhāo领lǐng处chù。但dàn是shì没méi有yǒu人rén丢diū企qǐ鹅é。
He went to the Lost and Found Office. But no one was missing a penguin.
他tā问wèn小xiǎo鸟niǎo，小xiǎo鸟niǎo不bù理lǐ他tā。他tā问wèn鸭yā子zi，鸭yā子zi漂piāo走zǒu了le。
He asked the birds, they ignored him. He asked the duck, the duck floated away.
男nán孩hái睡shuì不bù着zháo。他tā很hěn想xiǎng帮bāng忙máng，但dàn是shì他tā不bù知zhī道dào怎zěn么me办bàn。
The boy couldn't sleep. He wanted to help, but he didn't know how.

Scene 3 · The Journey 旅程

第dì二èr天tiān，他tā发fā现xiàn企qǐ鹅é住zhù在zài南Nán极jí。他tā造zào了le一yì艘sōu小xiǎo船chuán。
The next morning, he discovered penguins live at the South Pole. He made a small boat.
他tā们men划huá了le很hěn多duō天tiān。遇yù到dào大dà风fēng大dà浪làng，男nán孩hái一yì直zhí讲jiǎng故gù事shì给gěi企qǐ鹅é听tīng。
They rowed for many days. Through big storms, the boy told stories to the penguin.

Scene 4 · The Realisation 真相

到dào了le南Nán极jí，企qǐ鹅é看kàn起qǐ来lai很hěn伤shāng心xīn。男nán孩hái说shuō再zài见jiàn，划huá船chuán回huí家jiā。
At the South Pole, the penguin looked sad. The boy said goodbye and rowed home.
但dàn是shì男nán孩hái越yuè想xiǎng越yuè不bú对duì。企qǐ鹅é不bú是shì迷mí路lù了le，它tā只zhǐ是shì太tài孤gū单dān了le！
But the more he thought, the more he realised: The penguin wasn't lost. He was just lonely!
他tā赶gǎn快kuài划huá回huí去qù。终zhōng于yú找zhǎo到dào了le企qǐ鹅é，他tā们men一yì起qǐ开kāi心xīn地de回huí家jiā了le。
He quickly rowed back. He found the penguin, and they went home together happily.
Chinese Story Performance Project · Rehearsal Script
Huíjiā zhī Lǚ

回家之旅

The Journey Home

Scene 1 · The Melting Ice 融化的冰

北běi极jí熊xióng住zhù在zài冰bīng上shàng。可kě是shì冰bīng融róng化huà了le，食shí物wù也yě没méi有yǒu了le。
Polar Bear lived on the ice. But the ice was melting, and there was no food.
他tā说shuō：我wǒ不bù能néng留liú在zài这zhè里lǐ了le。他tā看kàn到dào一yì艘sōu小xiǎo船chuán，爬pá了le上shàng去qù。
He said: I can't stay here. He found a little boat and climbed in.

Scene 2 · The Panda 熊猫

他tā划huá到dào了le城chéng市shì。一yì只zhī熊xióng猫māo问wèn：你nǐ在zài干gàn什shén么me？
He sailed to a city. A panda asked: What are you doing?
北běi极jí熊xióng说shuō：我wǒ在zài找zhǎo新xīn家jiā。熊xióng猫māo说shuō：我wǒ也yě不bù能néng留liú在zài这zhè里lǐ，我wǒ跟gēn你nǐ一yì起qǐ走zǒu！
Polar Bear said: I'm looking for a new home. Panda said: I can't stay here either, I'll come with you!

Scene 3 · The Orang-utan & Elephant 猩猩和大象

他tā们men遇yù到dào了le红hóng毛máo猩xīng猩xing。她tā说shuō：树shù都dōu没méi有yǒu了le，我wǒ没méi地dì方fāng爬pá了le。
They met an orang-utan. She said: The trees are gone, I have nothing to climb.
他tā们men遇yù到dào一yì头tóu大dà象xiàng。大dà象xiàng说shuō：有yǒu人rén在zài偷tōu我wǒ的de象xiàng牙yá，我wǒ很hěn害hài怕pà。
They met an elephant. The elephant said: Someone is trying to steal my tusks, I am scared.
大dà家jiā说shuō：跟gēn我wǒ们men走zǒu吧ba！大dà象xiàng爬pá上shàng了le船chuán。
Everyone said: Come with us! The elephant climbed into the boat.

Scene 4 · The Dodo 渡渡鸟

暴bào风fēng雨yǔ过guò后hòu，他tā们men迷mí路lù了le。他tā们men看kàn到dào了le一yì只zhī渡dù渡dù鸟niǎo。
After a storm, they were lost. They saw a Dodo bird.
渡dù渡dù鸟niǎo说shuō：等děng树shù长zhǎng回huí来lái，冰bīng回huí来lái，城chéng市shì不bú再zài变biàn大dà，你nǐ们men就jiù可kě以yǐ回huí家jiā了le。
The Dodo said: When the trees grow back, the ice returns, and cities stop getting bigger, you can go home.

Kāihuā Dàlóu

开花大楼

Flower Block

Scene 1 · The Seeds 种子

杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà和hé妈mā妈ma种zhòng下xià了le向xiàng日rì葵kuí种zhǒng子zi。妈mā妈ma，接jiē下xià来lái呢ne？
Jeremiah and his mum planted sunflower seeds. Mum, now what?
妈mā妈ma说shuō：等děng待dài奇qí迹jì发fā生shēng。那nà天tiān晚wǎn上shang，杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà许xǔ了le一yí个ge愿yuàn。
Mum said: Wait for the magic to happen. That night, Jeremiah made a wish.

Scene 2 · The Magic 奇迹

第dì二èr天tiān早zǎo上shang，哥gē哥ge大dà喊hǎn：杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà，快kuài醒xǐng醒xǐng！出chū大dà事shì了le！
The next morning, his brother shouted: Jeremiah, wake up! Something huge happened!
藤téng蔓wàn和hé叶yè子zi穿chuān破pò了le天tiān花huā板bǎn！哥gē哥ge问wèn：我wǒ们men该gāi怎zěn么me办bàn？！
Vines and leaves burst through the ceiling! His brother asked: What are we going to do?!
杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà看kàn到dào了le爸bà爸ba的de照zhào片piàn。爸bà爸ba笑xiào着zhe说shuō：跟gēn我wǒ来lái！
Jeremiah saw his dad's photo. Dad smiled and said: Follow me!

Scene 3 · The Neighbours 邻居们

他tā们men跑pǎo上shàng楼lóu梯tī。每měi一yì层céng楼lóu都dōu有yǒu新xīn的de花huā和hé新xīn的de邻lín居jū。
They ran up the stairs. On every floor, there were new flowers and new neighbours.
大dà家jiā都dōu很hěn开kāi心xīn，一yì起qǐ帮bāng忙máng找zhǎo植zhí物wù的de顶dǐng端duān。
Everyone was happy, helping together to find the top of the plant.
杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà有yǒu点diǎn害hài怕pà。哥gē哥ge拍pāi拍pāi他tā的de肩jiān膀bǎng说shuō：别bié怕pà，有yǒu我wǒ在zài，爸bà爸ba也yě在zài。
Jeremiah was a bit scared. His brother patted his shoulder and said: Don't worry, I've got you, and so has Dad.

Scene 4 · The Key 钥匙

到dào了le顶dǐng楼lóu，门mén锁suǒ了le！邻lín居jū说shuō：62 号hào的de克Kè里lǐ普pǔ先xiān生sheng有yǒu钥yào匙shi。
At the top floor, the door was locked! A neighbour said: Mr Crisp in Flat 62 has the key.
杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà很hěn害hài怕pà克Kè里lǐ普pǔ先xiān生sheng。但dàn他tā还hái是shì去qù敲qiāo门mén了le。
Jeremiah was scared of Mr Crisp. But he went and knocked on the door anyway.
克Kè里lǐ普pǔ先xiān生sheng开kāi门mén了le。他tā没méi有yǒu生shēng气qì，反fǎn而ér笑xiào了le！他tā说shuō：这zhè些xiē叶yè子zi让ràng我wǒ想xiǎng起qǐ了le小xiǎo时shí候hou爬pá树shù！
Mr Crisp opened the door. He wasn't angry, he laughed! He said: These leaves remind me of climbing trees when I was a boy!

Scene 5 · The Roof Garden 屋顶花园

门mén开kāi了le。哇wa！屋wū顶dǐng变biàn成chéng了le天tiān堂táng！到dào处chù都dōu是shì花huā，中zhōng间jiān是shì杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà的de向xiàng日rì葵kuí。
The door opened. Wow! The roof had become a paradise! Flowers everywhere, and in the middle was Jeremiah's sunflower.
杰Jié里lǐ迈mài亚yà对duì大dà家jiā说shuō：我wǒ的de植zhí物wù让ràng我wǒ们men开kāi心xīn。我wǒ们men要yào在zài这zhè里lǐ种zhòng更gèng多duō的de花huā，为wèi了le爸bà爸ba，也yě为wèi了le我wǒ们men想xiǎng念niàn的de人rén。
Jeremiah said to everyone: My plant has made us happy. We should plant more flowers here, for Dad, and for everyone we miss.
邻lín居jū们men欢huān呼hū起qǐ来lai。从cóng那nà天tiān起qǐ，他tā们men一yì起qǐ建jiàn造zào了le美měi丽lì的de开kāi花huā大dà楼lóu。
The neighbours cheered. From that day on, they built a beautiful Flower Block together.
